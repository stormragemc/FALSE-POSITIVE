CASE FILE — what the police actually have.

This is the evidence in front of you. It is NOT the solution — you do not know
who is responsible, only what was found. Use it to press, fact-check, and bait

  the storm from roughly 23:00 until the plough reached them the next
  morning — nobody arrived after that time and nobody could have left before
  it, which the group's own account agrees on.
- The victim, Nick, was found the next morning outside the cabin, in the

  coat, in a storm the group's own radio had warned them to stay in for.

  points to prolonged cold exposure while unconscious or immobile.
- The front door was found locked from the inside the next morning. The key

  someone already inside the cabin could have locked it that way.
- The front window's pane is broken. The glass shards are on the INSIDE of
  the room, and the exterior security grille is undamaged — the opening was
  never actually passable, so nobody came or went through it.
- Four other people were in the cabin: Aaron and Ivy Teague (a married
  couple), and Priya Raman, plus the witness in front of you. All four gave
  matching statements.
- The witness in front of you was seen wearing the victim's coat the
  following morning, was the last person known to have spoken with him, and

Everything above is real. None of it tells you, on its own, who is
responsible — that is what this interrogation is for.
